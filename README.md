## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a principal-level engineer with **11+ years** of experience delivering cloud-native distributed systems across healthcare, enterprise SaaS and data platforms. Most recently I was a **Principal Member of Technical Staff at Oracle Health Data Intelligence**, where I led architecture for AI-enabled healthcare systems.

My sweet spot is the space between a messy real-world problem and a production system: sitting with product, partners and engineers, translating what the business actually needs into a secure, scalable design, and then building and shipping it. That is why I'm aiming for **Forward Deployed Engineer** roles, where deep engineering meets close work with customers.

- 🤖 **AI engineering:** LLM-powered apps, Model Context Protocol (MCP), RAG, agentic workflows and reusable AI skills
- 🔐 **Application security:** OAuth 2.0, OIDC, PKCE, DPoP, Spring Security, vulnerability governance and remediation
- 🏗️ **System design:** microservices, event-driven and serverless architectures, API design, performance and cost optimization
- 🚀 **Scalable, secure AI-native apps:** from semantic search to AI agents for operations, designed with security and reliability from day one
